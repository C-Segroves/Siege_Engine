# Playtest findings, 2026-10-11 (second round, user report)

Each item has an owner agent. Mark items FIXED with the test that covers them.

## X. Extraction mode (owner F; stations with H; packs with C)
- X1 Dropped gear floats too high above the ground to interact with. **FIXED (F)**: a bag or dropped item lands on the first floor below where it fell (building floor, roof, else terrain). The ray ignores soldiers' and vehicles' bodies, which it used to land on (`ExtractionDirector.make_drop_point`). Test: `tests/extraction_check.tscn` (X1).
- X2 Inventory terminals do nothing: they should open a menu to spend credits (buy gear) or reload ammo. **FIXED (F, with H)**: the supply and inventory panels are modals (centred, the HUD dimmed under a ModalShade, rows in two columns with wrapping names). In a raid an inventory station (TribesStations, H) refills health and energy and opens the **supply panel** (`ExtractionStation`, `StationShopPanel`). It has **Reload** from carried ammo, plus **Buy** for ammunition loads for the weapons in hand, the armour's grenades, mines and repair kits, and the weapons it may carry, at shop prices. Credits carried in the raid are spent first, then the pilot's account. It is host-checked: the soldier must stand on a working station, have the money and have room. Test: `tests/extraction_check.tscn` (X2), `renders/extraction2_x2_station_panel.png`.
- X3 Packs don't work: an ammo pack brought in, R pressed, nothing happened while out of ammo. **FIXED (C)**: a pack worn from the inventory now raises the weapons' loads (`Infantry.apply_worn_pack`; it never did when worn after the weapons were equipped). R (`pack_use`) switches a toggled pack (shield, repair, jammer) and otherwise reloads right away from the carried ammo (`InfantryGearDirector.reload_hook`, extraction: `ExtractionLoadout.apply_reload`). With no ammo carried there's nothing to load. Whether an ammo-pack item carries its own rounds is left to F. Test: `tests/fix_c_check.tscn` (X3). F's decision: a pack item carries no rounds of its own. In a raid ammunition is always its own `ammo/` stack. The ammo pack raises how much the weapons hold, and the supply station (X2) sells loads.
- X4 Select items in your inventory to equip them if not equipped already. **FIXED (F)**: the inventory panel marks `[IN HAND]`, `[READY]` (in a weapon slot) and `[WORN]`. Its **Equip** button (mouse, or the pad focus chain plus A) takes a weapon in hand or wears a pack. It works by moving the item to the front of the inventory, which the loadout reads in order (`ExtractionDirector.request_equip`, host). Test: `tests/extraction_check.tscn` (X4), `renders/extraction2_x4_inventory_equip.png`.
- X5 The Tribes 1 Blaster is always free (a default weapon if you run out of money) and has no value. Tribes 2 blasters are slightly better in this mode if they aren't already, and cost money. **FIXED (F)**: rules `free_weapons` (`tribes:BLASTER`) makes it price 0 standard issue. A raider who brings no weapon his armour can carry is given one. It is never rolled as loot and never left in a death bag. The Tribes 2 blaster is a paid raid upgrade: rules `weapon_overrides` give it damage x1.2, refire x0.9 (2000 vs the free blaster's 1250 damage per second; 1500 untuned) and a price of 450 cr (`ExtractionLoadout.apply_weapon_overrides` on every equip). Test: `tests/extraction_check.tscn` (X5).
- X6 The third-person view on the transport coming into the level is shaky. **FIXED (F)**: a passenger's chase camera followed his soldier-sized rig inside the moving hull. The sphere sweep pulled it into the hull every frame. It now follows the vehicle he rides in, with the vehicle rig (`BattleCameras.make_follow_body`). The project uses no physics interpolation, and the camera eases at render rate on the transport's transform. Test: `tests/extraction_check.tscn` (X6), `renders/extraction2_x6_dropin_camera.png`.
- X7 No ejecting until the transport is over a legal area; if the area would kill the player, they can't get out yet. **FIXED (F)**: the host asks `ExitSafety` before a raid rider gets out (`OccupancyDirector.exit_guard`, which also covers the jump key). The exit spot must be inside the mission area, over a floor whose fall his armour survives at his health, not over liquid (MapWater), and not inside geometry. Otherwise the request is refused and he sees "Wait - not safe to drop: <reason>" (`exit_refused` -> notify). Test: `tests/extraction_check.tscn` (X7).
- X8 Pre-mission tasks to pick from, each with its own credit value. Examples: steal the flag from a given faction (when the map has CTF), blow up a given faction's generator, kill N of a faction's infantry or N of its vehicles. **FIXED (F)**: rules `tasks` (data-driven templates, board size 4, take up to 2; reward = reward_per x count x faction difficulty) feed the stash screen's "Raid tasks" board (Take / Drop; saved in the profile and carried in the roster kit). In the raid (`RaidTasks`, host) the task factions are placed first. Kills by the player, on foot or from his vehicle, count. A generator task binds the TribesBase generator nearest the faction's area. Generator and flag tasks claim one of the map's bases (G's `mode_objects`: a team's generators and flag) for their faction, whose squad then guards the loot area nearest that base. A flag task puts the faction flag (a loot item in the mode-object flag's shape) at that flag; carrying it out completes the task and hands the flag in (it is never stashed). A live raid on Katabatic tests both: the generator blown up, the flag carried out, 5400 cr paid. Tasks the map can't hold are void. Progress goes to its owner, appears in the ObjectivesPanel as `Kill 1 Blood Eagle soldiers - 150 cr`, and is paid on extraction. Test: `tests/extraction_check.tscn` (X8), `renders/extraction2_x8_task_done_panel.png`.

## W. Weapons (owner C)
- W1 The Tribes 2 Spinfusor model looks half cut off: check whether that is the whole model. **FIXED (C)**: two causes. ## G. General (owners B, C)
- G1 Check out-of-bounds warning/kill areas so underground buildings aren't too far down (B). **FIXED** (`engine/map_bounds.gd`: the kill floor is 100 m below the lowest terrain sample *or building / prop / structure* of the map; at a point the floor is 100 m below the lower of the heightfield surface (terrain holes included) and the bottoms of buildings over it that reach below that surface, so a bridge or floating base never raises it (fixes F's Katabatic surprise deaths); fell-through deaths respect spawn protection and read "fell out of the world"; soldiers use the battle's rule instead of their own terrain-only check; the mission area stays the 2-D map bounds, so depth never warns. Test `tests/bounds_maps_check.tscn`: all 164 maps, lowest building above the floor, a 5x5 grid of terrain points under every building footprint is never "fallen through", and no start (incl. underground drop points: KingUnderTheHill 100 m, AntHill / FreeForAll 95 m, Rasp 86 m below the terrain) counts as fallen through)
- G2 Hard landings for infantry do too much damage: deaths from what felt like small drops (C). **FIXED (C)**: Tribes 1 counts only the speed above minDamageSpeed (tribes.exe 0x4162d2: (speed − 25) × damageScale), but the remake counted the whole speed, about 3–6× too much near the threshold. Tribes 2 matches Armor::onImpact (whole speed above minImpactSpeed 45). Drops from rest (20 m/s²): T1 hurts from 15.6 m and kills from 616 m (light); T2 hurts from 50.6 m and kills from 681 m (light). Impact speed is measured along the surface normal, as in both engines. Test: `tests/fix_c_check.tscn` (G2, per-armour table). The surprise deaths themselves came from the remake's stuck-soldier rule, which killed a soldier 6 s after he came to rest on faces too steep to stand on (a gully or a crease), with the message "killed by the environment". It now kills only a soldier whose body is inside the geometry, and the message says so ("got stuck in the scenery"; falling out of the world reads "died out of bounds"). Probe: `tests/damage_probe.tscn` logs every damage event on every soldier through idle, slope and skiing, 5–20 m drops, roof, ceiling-jet, vehicle exit and bot-match scenarios on Raindance and Katabatic. It found no other surprise; the remaining fall hits are bots coming down from jets above the threshold.
