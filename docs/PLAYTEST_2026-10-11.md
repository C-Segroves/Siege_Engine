# Playtest findings, 2026-10-11 (user report)

Each item has an owner group (A–E) for the fix round. Mark items FIXED with the test that covers them.

## A. Aiming, targeting, AI fire, sensors
- A1 Some Hercs (e.g. Starsiege Prometheus, Starsiege Hercs in general) don't shoot where the crosshair is. **FIXED**: one sight per vehicle (`Herc.aim_basis` = chassis + torso twist + pitch, `aim_origin` = the eye point); the cockpit camera is turned to it every frame (`BattleCameras.apply_sight`), so the crosshair (projection centre) is the aim ray; every muzzle and multi-beam converges on the aim point (`Weapon.make_aim_point`). Before: aim along the head0/turret rest frame from the cockpit node, up to 150 m off at 300 m. Test: `aim_convergence_check` (104 vehicles x 3 torso poses x 40/120/300 m, worst miss 0.17 m).
- A2 Targeting cycles through everything on the map; it should prefer what is roughly in front of the player. **FIXED**: `Targeting` (engine/targeting.gd): target_nearest = nearest the crosshair (angle off the sight, distance-weighted, hidden ones penalised); target_next cycles within the 30-degree front cone in view, all candidates only when none is in front; targetable structures (group "targetables") included; non-Herc targets work in every target display (`TargetInfo`). Test: `fix_a_check` A2.
- A3 Not every Herc can move its torso/turret (ES1 Hercs do it with right mouse); check all others. **FIXED**: every ground vehicle takes the same aim input (mouse / right stick) within its limits; full-turn turrets (tanks) wrap and turn the short way (`Herc.make_torso_yaw_step`, `PlayerPilot.make_turned_yaw`); the cockpit view now turns with the torso on every vehicle (Starsiege eye nodes did not). Test: `fix_a_check` A3 (all pilotable ground vehicles), `torso_check`.
- A4 ES1 campaign wingmen try to shoot through hills (no line-of-sight check). **FIXED**: `LineOfSight` ray from the eye; `AIPilot` holds fire and repositions (closes in while side-stepping) when blocked, `FlyerAI` holds fire. Test: `fix_a_check` A4.
- A5 Teams must be identifiable, especially when targeting a teammate; names above teammates' heads. **FIXED**: `TargetInfo` friend / enemy / neutral words and colours in the ES1 target box, ES2 brackets, MFD target page, Tribes marker, plain HUD and radars; `Nameplates` billboard names (LAN names) over allied units, friend-coloured, faded 150-700 m. Test: `fix_a_check` A5; renders/fix_a_es1_friend.png, fix_a_soldier_hud.png.
- A6 Radar: teammate and enemy positions must match where they really are. **FIXED**: one frame for all radars (`HeadingFrame`: heading from the sight, so a flyer's bank no longer rotated its scope); plain HUD (vehicles and soldiers), ES MFD scanner and Starsiege scope use it. Test: `fix_a_check` A6 (known placements under turn, twist and bank).
- A7 Compass waypoints for every vehicle and infantry. **FIXED**: ES1/ES2 tapes use `HeadingFrame`; Tribes compass dials get a waypoint dot; HUDs without a compass (Starsiege) and the plain HUD (soldiers, vehicles without a cockpit) get the remake's `HudCompass` tape with the waypoint marker and distance. Test: `fix_a_check` A7 (every pilotable vehicle's cockpit has a compass); renders/fix_a_starsiege_hud.png.
- A8 ES1 spider mines must explode when close enough to their target. **FIXED** per the simulators (gameplay.md section 2.1): the ES1 Spider / ES2 Arachnitron walk / lunge (3x pace) cycle, detonation on contact (DAT 0x6e radii, or a base), 2000 damage within 3000 units on about half the components with linear fall-off per component (ES2: facing shield first, never itself), no blast when shot; rules read from DBSIM at import (tools/self_destruct_rules.py). Starsiege ARAC spider mines crawl at their target and go off on contact; Battledrome chase mines chase within their detect radius and go off when shot (`MineRunner`, `MineBody`; no remake minimums). Tests: `spider_check`, `fix_a_check` A8.

## B. Collisions, world, teams, structures
- B1 Getting run over damages infantry; vehicle collisions damage vehicles. **FIXED** (`engine/collision_damage.gd`: ES1 ramming rule DBSIM 0x14180 / 0x13a7c for vehicle<->vehicle and vehicle<->structure, Tribes 2 collDamageThresholdVel / collDamageMultiplier for running over soldiers, kills credited, host only; test `tests/fix_b_check.tscn` B1)
- B2 Map boundaries: kill players too far out of bounds, or who clip through the ground and keep falling. **FIXED** (`engine/map_bounds.gd` area from map bounds / heightfield / ES ground plane; `games/es1/battle/battle_bounds.gd`: Tribes dm.cs rule, 3 warnings 1.5 s apart, then 0.05/0.66 of health per second; below the world floor = dead, respawn per mode; starts outside the area are skipped; test `fix_b_check` B2)
- B3 Option for more teams, only for maps that support more (e.g. Four Way Dance). **FIXED** (data/API: `engine/map_teams.gd` MapTeams.make_team_count / make_team_color / make_team_name; MapBuilder `team_spawns`; config `team_count` + `teams`: BattleSpawner starts/respawns each team at its own starts, BattleRules team frag limit; UI option is D12; test `fix_b_check` B3)
- B4 All campaign buildings destructible (at least mission targets) and targetable. **FIXED** (Structure / SSStructure join group `targetables` with display_name / team / alive / integrity, integrity and destruction synced to LAN clients; Starsiege neutral scenery and indestructible props stay out; target cycling of the group is agent A's targeting; test `fix_b_check` B4 on ES1 B0_C0_0A, ES2 C1_01, Starsiege HA4)

## C. Infantry runtime (Tribes)
- C1 Tribes 2 infantry have no death animation. **FIXED**: the armour `.dsq` animations are exported (`torque_dts.read_dsq`, TSShapeConstructor names: run/back/side/fall/jet/land, death1-11) and played; a dead soldier plays one of his body's deaths, the same on every peer. Test: `tests/fix_c_check.tscn` (C1), render `renders/fix_c_poses.png`.
- C2 Check the weapon animations for Tribes guns. **FIXED**: both weapon models play the image sequences by role (T2 state machine Activate / Ready / Fire / Reload / NoAmmo stateSequence, ambient, spin; T1 activation / fire / reload / ambient / spin) at their authored speed. Test: `tests/fix_c_check.tscn` (C2).
- C3 Tribes 2 training: footsteps too fast and too loud. **FIXED**: one step per foot trigger of the run cycle (cycle speed capped at Torque maxTimeScale 1.5: ~4 steps/s at full run, was 8.75), left/right soft/hard sounds with the originals' profiles (T2 AudioClosest3d 5-30 m, T1 Profile3dFoot 2-30 m) instead of 40 m Herc footsteps. Test: `tests/fix_c_check.tscn` (C3).
- C4 Tribes 2 training: items to pick up (e.g. sniper rifle) were not there; need an interact button that also picks items up. **FIXED**: items of every Tribes map exported (`tools/export_tribes_items.py`) and drawn (`TribesPickups`; training: `TribesItemModels`), taken by touch as in Tribes; one `interact` action (E / pad X: items, loot, switches, stations, hatches; `loot_pickup` removed). Tests: `tests/fix_c_check.tscn` (C4), `tests/tribes_training_check.tscn` (items drawn), `tests/input_check.tscn`, `tests/extraction_check.tscn`; render `renders/fix_c_pickup_offer.png`.
- C5 With no Tribes pack installed: Hercs only, pilots can't get out. **FIXED**: no enabled infantry pack -> pilots stay in, no exit hint (`OccupancyDirector.can_leave_vehicle`); one pack installed -> that one. Test: `tests/fix_c_check.tscn` (C5).

## D. Multiplayer flow and UI
- D1 Infantry bots don't work when added while hosting. **FIXED**: the host's bot count reaches the battle config and every peer (LAN e2e `--bots`: both peers see the bots move, deathmatch, co-op and team versus). The broken case was a co-op campaign: missions spawn no bots, but the host screen offered the slider. It is now hidden there with an explanation, and the settings carry 0. Bots are dealt round all the teams of a 3- or 4-team match (`InfantryBots.make_team` uses `team_count`). Tests: `fix_d_check` (host -> config), e2e lan_host/lan_join `--bots=2`.
- D2 Host/Join buttons only appear after Arena → Back. **FIXED**: the library's Play section has Host LAN game and Join LAN game from the start (launcher start modes "host" / "join"). Test: `fix_d_check` D2.
- D3 Host can start while players aren't ready (kicks them); require all ready. **FIXED**: the host's Start match / Start mission button stays disabled until every connected pilot is ready, and the footer names who isn't (`MatchFlow.try_start_hosted_match`, `Net.make_unready_names`). The pilot list shows READY / not ready. A rematch from the results screen still starts at once, since everybody is on that screen. --autohost waits for ready and --autojoin marks itself ready. Tests: `fix_d_check`, e2e lan (Start disabled until the client is ready).
- D4 Co-op campaign: clearer instructions to start; a Ready button. **FIXED**: numbered steps in the lobby (`LobbyText`) for the host (pick a Herc, wait for READY, Start mission) and the clients (pick from the host's garage, press Ready bottom right), plus a hint on the host screen. Clients have Ready / Not ready, and the host's button reads Start mission. Tests: `fix_d_check`, e2e lan campaign; renders/fix_d_lobby_campaign_client.png.
- D5 Player names saved locally. **FIXED**: `PlayerProfile` keeps the name in settings.json. `NameRow` edits it in the launcher menu, the lobby (which also renames you in the roster live) and Options, and `Net.player_name` loads it at start; chat and nameplates use the roster name. Test: `fix_d_check` D5.
- D6 Infantry armours selectable in arena and versus. **FIXED**: `PilotPicker` ("Your soldier": starting armour, body, skin) in the armory / skirmish, the lobby (versus, co-op) and Options. The choice goes into the roster as infantry_armor / infantry_body / infantry_skin, which agent C's BattleInfantry spawns from. The parked Herc is still chosen above it. Tests: `fix_d_check`; renders/fix_d_lobby_vs.png, fix_d_armory.png.
- D7 Choose infantry game (Tribes 1 or 2); Tribes 2 adds a Bioderm light-armour pilot option. **FIXED**: Infantry game row (only when both are installed) in Options, the armory and the host screen (config "infantry_pack"). The Body row lists InfantryCatalog.make_bodies, which includes the Tribes 2 Bioderm. With no Tribes installed, every infantry row and the bots row are hidden. Test: `fix_d_check`.
- D8 Skin selection where a model has skins. **FIXED**: tools/export_skins.py extracts the alternate skins from the discs into gamedata/<pack>/skins/skins.json (Tribes 2: 7 per human armour, 2 per Bioderm; Tribes: 8 per armour; Starsiege: 250 Herc skins over 40 Hercs). `SkinCatalog` reads them, and `ShapeInstance.texture_overrides` draws them. Armour skin row in PilotPicker; Skin row in HercSelector (saved per Herc, roster "herc_skin" -> spawn spec "skin" -> Herc). The hangar previews both. Test: `fix_d_check` D8.
- D9 Main menu UI with a Tribes 2 look (colour scheme, translucent panels), no T2 textures. **FIXED**: `siege_theme.gd` only: teal / cyan-green on near-black with a generated glow, translucent panels with thin bright borders and a bright left edge, header-bar section labels, a teal title bar, amber highlights; the content column is a translucent panel (`ContentPanel`); the 3D hangar is kept. Screens: renders/fix_d_library.png, fix_d_options.png, fix_d_saves.png, fix_d_briefing_es1.png, fix_d_extraction.png, fix_d_host_*.png, fix_d_lobby_*.png.
